# Earth Data Methodology

## Study locations
Atacama, Ladakh and Iceland.

## Data products
The workflow uses MODIS land-surface temperature and
NASA POWER daily near-surface air-temperature data.

## Study period
The current summary covers July 2024.

## Processing
The workflow includes daily temperature analysis,
day/night coverage analysis, paired day-night comparisons
and MODIS quality-flag diagnostics.

## Limitations
MODIS LST measures surface temperature, not air temperature.
NASA POWER T2M is a gridded near-surface air-temperature product.
Their spatial resolution and sampling differ.
