4. EARTH-MARS COMPARABILITY
---------------------------
Two comparison tracks must remain distinct:

A. Surface:
   MODIS LST versus MEDA TIRS ground temperature.

B. Atmospheric:
   POWER T2M versus MEDA TIRS air temperature.

Surface and air temperature are not interchangeable.

6. SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
-------------------------
- Earth data currently represent July 2024, not annual climatology.
- Iceland has substantially lower MODIS coverage.
- Sol-wise Mars profiles are not seasonal profiles without verified Ls.
- Temperature similarity alone does not establish habitat suitability.
- Radiation, pressure, dust, terrain, water and resources remain
  separate analogue dimensions.
